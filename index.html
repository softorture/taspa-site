    Content is the slow part of paid social.

    [FAQ]

    Questions brands ask first.

    Who owns the videos?

    You get the right to use them in ads on any platform, with no time limit. There is no separate license to renew, and no creator who can pull out.

    Do I need to label the ads as AI?

    Several platforms ask advertisers to disclose realistic AI-generated people. We tell you which setting to turn on in each ad manager and keep a small AI note in the end card if you want one.

    How many revisions do I get?

    Two rounds per video are included. Each extra round is $75.

    What do you need from me?

    A link to the product, a few clear photos from different angles, and where the ad will run. If you have brand rules or past ads, send those too.

    How do I pay?

    Half before we start and half before the clean final. Cards through a payment link, or a USD bank transfer for larger orders.

    How fast is it?

    First draft in 72 hours for up to 3 videos. Bigger packs take 5 to 7 days, brand films 7 to 14 days. Rush delivery is +30%.

    What if I don't like the result?

    Start with the free sample, so you see our work on your product before you pay. On paid work, the two revision rounds cover changes.

    Do you work with agencies?

    Yes. We can deliver unbranded files under your agency and keep to your client's brief and approval steps.
